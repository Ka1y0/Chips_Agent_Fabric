# Code of Conduct

CHIPS Agent Fabric welcomes constructive participation from people with varied
backgrounds, identities, abilities, and levels of experience.

Participants must communicate respectfully, critique ideas and code rather than
people, protect private information, acknowledge uncertainty and provenance,
and help keep collaboration safe. Harassment, threats, discrimination,
doxxing, deliberate credential exposure, or sustained disruptive behavior are
not acceptable.

Maintainers may clarify expectations, remove harmful content, reject changes,
or restrict participation when needed to protect the community and project.
Reports should be made privately through the security/contact method published
by the canonical repository host. Until a public contact is established, do not
publish sensitive reports; preserve them locally and notify a maintainer through
an already trusted private channel.

Enforcement should be proportionate, documented where privacy permits, and
focused on safety and restoration. Good-faith mistakes should be met with clear
guidance; repeated or severe violations may require stronger action.
